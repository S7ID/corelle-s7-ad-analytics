What the Data Says

💰 MER 14.8× — the store runs on demand

Blended MER (₹15.4L revenue ÷ ₹1.04L spend) is ~3× Meta's attributed 4.52×. Paid is only ~7% of revenue — an accelerant on strong organic/repeat demand. Read MER for the business; use Meta ROAS only to steer delivery.

🚨 We are dark — every day costs ~₹7,900

No paid media since 31 Jul. Revenue has fallen each day off: ₹25,703 → ₹16,394 → ₹13,414. At the measured lift of ₹7,922/day, 3 dark days ≈ ₹23,800 foregone. The 1 Aug relaunch has not been built — this is now the most expensive open item on the account.

🏁 FINAL: blended MER 5.05×

₹6,25,092 store revenue ÷ ₹1,23,758 spend over 22 days, 144 orders, AOV ₹3,855. Meta ROAS 3.21×. Closed 12% under budget (88% of ₹1.4L) and delivered 80% of the ₹14L target — shortfall driven by the 21-day late start and Google never being funded, not by delivery.

❌ Retargeting audiences did NOT populate — correction

Three days after creation the new audiences still report 20 people with status "Normal". I earlier called this the campaign's biggest unlock; on the evidence that was premature and it has not materialised. The pixel itself is healthy (~24k PageViews/day, Purchase EMQ 9.3), and every legacy website audience shows the same 20, so the fault is in website-audience building on this account, not in tracking. Verify directly in Ads Manager → Audiences before building any 1 Aug plan on retargeting — the API count may under-report, but it cannot be assumed working.

✅ Audit: account is clean, no spend leakage

Scanned all 140 campaigns and 444 ad sets. Exactly two are active — both Monsoon Mania — and they account for 100% of account spend (₹1,03,994, matching the account total to the rupee). Every legacy 2023–24 campaign is correctly paused. Both live ad sets carry the 31 Jul end date, so nothing can overrun into August.

✅ Executed 27 Jul — image reverted to ₹4,500/day

The scale-up test is closed: ₹6,500 → ₹4,500/day, back to the proven efficient point where image delivered 3.7–3.8×. Catalogue holds at ₹1,500 (trimmed, never stopped). Total ₹6,000/day for the final 4 days. Rationale: at ₹6,500 the extra spend returned ~1.5×; at ₹4,500 the same money historically returns 3.7×, so reverting earns more revenue on less spend — the actual definition of maximising revenue when a channel is saturated.

📡 Platform coverage: Meta only

Google Ads never ran — never funded, and there is no Google Ads API access connected here (Supermetrics would cover it but needs authorisation), so it cannot be audited, only reported as zero. 100% of paid media this campaign was Meta. The brief assumed a 70/30 Meta/Google split, so roughly 30% of planned media never existed — the main structural reason the ₹14L target falls short. Funding Google on day one is the biggest single change available for 1 Aug.

🚀 Relaunch plan — ready, now 3 days overdue

Evidence-based priorities: (1) fix the Shopify→Meta inventory sync (1,431 of 1,616 SKUs out of stock) — unlocks the best engine at zero media cost; (2) lead with catalogue/DPA and invert the budget split; (3) fund Google; (4) all-new image creative incl. the mandated video; (5) value-optimised ad set so budget follows revenue not order count; (6) verify retargeting audiences; (7) geo holdout to measure true lift.

🧮 Incremental MER revised UP to 1.41× — now with a post-period

Going dark produced something the campaign could not: a second, clean baseline. Store averaged ₹22,304/day before ads (19 Jun–9 Jul) and ₹18,504/day after (1–3 Aug) — a combined ads-off baseline of ₹21,829/day vs ₹29,751/day while running. That is a lift of ₹7,922/day → 1.41× incremental MER, up from the 1.32× pre-only estimate. A pre and post comparison is a materially stronger design than pre-only, though still not a controlled holdout.

🗂️ Catalogue feed is 88% dead — biggest fixable asset

Feed diagnostics flag 1,431 of ~1,616 products OUT OF STOCK (must-fix), plus 108 missing images, 13 missing descriptions, 1 missing title. Out-of-stock items cannot serve in dynamic ads at all. This is why the catalogue engine had to be hand-scoped to 183 SKUs. Fixing the Shopify→Meta inventory sync before 1 Aug would widen the sellable catalogue several-fold — the single highest-leverage non-creative fix available.

🏆 Catalogue is the winner — 3 straight gains

Catalogue ROAS has climbed 3.20× → 3.37× → 3.59× across the last three reads while image slid 3.35× → 3.19× → 3.17×. Catalogue CTR 6.59% is the best on the account. It does this on ₹1,500/day vs image ₹4,500/day — i.e. the best engine is the least funded. Caution: frequency is 3.43, so it is working a small pool hard — the 183-SKU cap and the dead feed are the binding constraints. Lead 1 Aug with catalogue/DPA.

🎯 ₹14L target closed at 80%

₹11,22,909 booked (19 Jun–31 Jul) against the ₹14L goal. Causes are structural and documented: campaign launched 21 days late and Google Ads was never funded, so 30% of the planned media mix never ran. Spend finished at ₹1,23,758 — 88% of budget.

🔧 The spend mix was structural, not a knob

Pausing Samosa worked, but Meta never funded Momos (₹261 all campaign at 9.66×) — its ad set optimises for purchase count, so it floods high-CTR / low-AOV Morning Blue (50% of spend, 3.60×) and starves the low-volume winner. Manual nudges couldn't beat the algorithm. The fix for next flight is a value-optimised ad set — needs ~50 value events/week (we ended at ~40) and a fresh learning phase, so it must start on day one, not mid-campaign.

🌱 A warm account, relaunched

Spend fell to ₹1,073 in the last 30 days on a proven 4.52× base. Monsoon Mania restarts the engine at the ideal moment — no cold-start.

📈 Fund the young

25–34 returns 8.06× on ₹4.2K while 35–44 (₹12.6K) drags at 3.18×. Shifting budget younger is the single clearest efficiency lever.

🎯 Feed first

Feed = 74% of spend at 4.93× ROAS. Keep Stories/Reels for cheap reach & CTR, but don't let them pull budget from Feed. Cut or fix FB Reels (1.57×).

📱 Weight iPhone

iPhone converts at 4.86× vs Android's 3.74×, even though Android clicks more. Value-based bidding will naturally lean iOS.

👩 Women-led

Women are 80% of spend, 8.4% CTR, 4.60× ROAS. The creative and offer are landing with the core buyer — lean in.

🔌 Close the data gap

Only Meta is wired up. Connecting Corelle's own GA4, Clarity & Shopify moves this from Meta-attributed to true blended ROAS.
